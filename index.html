What is EveryCorner?

Unlike traditional social media, EveryCorner is not about individual profiles. Your group is your identity. Friends, teams, communities, and organizations can create a Corner that everyone follows together.

How It Works

1. Create a Corner

Make a shared space for your friends, community, or team.

2. Invite People

Add members who help build your Corner.

3. Grow Together

Your Corner gets followers, not individual popularity.

What is EveryCorner?

EveryCorner changes social media by focusing on communities instead of individual profiles. Your group is your identity. Friends, teams, clubs, and communities can build a Corner that everyone follows together.

How EveryCorner Works

Create a Corner

Make a shared space for your friends, community, team, or organization.

Invite Members

Bring people together and let everyone help build the Corner.

Grow Together

Your Corner gets followers as a group instead of individuals competing.

A New Way To Connect

No popularity contests. No single person being the whole identity. EveryCorner is about communities, friendships, and shared experiences.
